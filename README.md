THESDEL

The Student Digital Exchange Layer

Learn. Grow. Become.

THESDEL is a student-focused digital platform built to make everyday academic and campus life more connected, organized, and useful.

From keeping track of timetables and classes to staying connected with classmates and managing important student activities, THESDEL brings the things that matter to students into one digital layer.

What We're Building

Learning

Tools that help students stay organized and make better use of their academic time.

Timetables

A connected timetable that provides context for what matters throughout the day.

Classes

Digital spaces where students can join their classes, communicate, share updates, and stay connected.

Attendance

A simple way for students to keep track of their attendance and academic participation.

Student Communication

Keeping important class information, announcements, and updates where students can actually find them.

Opportunities

Building pathways for students to discover useful opportunities and participate in the digital economy.

Our Vision

We believe student technology should go beyond simply putting existing school processes online.

THESDEL is being built as a digital layer for student life — connecting the academic, social, and practical parts of being a student.

Starting in Nigeria.
Building for Africa.

Built at OAU

THESDEL began as a student-built project at Obafemi Awolowo University.

We're building, testing, and learning directly from the students we serve.

Technology

THESDEL is built with modern web technologies and cloud infrastructure, with a focus on scalability, reliability, and a great experience across devices.
